YEOEUM Privacy Policy
Release preparation draft · Revised November 2, 2026
Operated by 크리티컬 리스닝 커뮤니티 · Developed by Mingkoo
Contact: yeoeum@anotier.art

1. Information processed on your device

YEOEUM records hand claps and surrounding sound through the microphone to estimate reverberation time by frequency band. Recordings may contain conversations or other personal information; review them before sharing. Measurement results, original recordings, analysis diagnostics, measurement time, device and audio settings are stored on your device. Place names and notes you enter, and optional space photos you take, are also stored. The app does not request location permission or collect GPS location.

2. Sharing outside your device

This version has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, automatic upload to a developer server, or app-operated cloud synchronization. Ordinary CSV and JSON exports do not attach audio or photos. JSON includes analysis curves and diagnostic information. Shared result images include the place, notes, measurement time, results, notices and optional photo shown in the preview. Diagnostic ZIP packages include original recordings and are shared through a separate user action. Information you send to apps and services through the system share sheet is handled under those services’ policies.

3. Photos and permissions

Microphone permission is requested when starting a measurement; camera permission is requested when you choose to take a photo. You can measure and share results without photos. A separate custom reverb inquiry requires a space photo for skin design. Captured photos are re-encoded for app storage without copying the original capture EXIF metadata. The app does not request access to your entire photo library. Choosing to save a result image to Photos requests add-only access; existing photos are not read. Sharing filters apply to the result image, while the attached space photo is stored separately. Permissions can be changed in iPhone Settings.

4. Retention, backups and deletion

Completed results and photos remain on your device until deleted. Original recordings and diagnostics are retained for inspection and reanalysis. Failed attempts, including missed claps and timeouts, and cancelled or interrupted attempts retain audio captured up to that point and available diagnostics, such as failure reasons and detection times. If permission is denied or recording never starts, diagnostics may remain without audio. App-managed record, photo and Captures folders are configured to be excluded from operating-system backups. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Copies you save to Files or other apps are not covered by this setting.

Deleting a record from history also deletes its linked photo and original files. Data management lets you delete original files for selected records or all app-managed data, including older experimental and cancelled recordings. The app cannot delete copies you have exported or sent by email. Storage errors can cause an incomplete deletion; follow the app’s message and retry.

5. Inquiries and changes

Your language preference is stored on your device. A custom reverb inquiry prepares an email with a ZIP of original audio and measurement data, or lets you save the package. The attached space photo is included for skin design. You must send the materials yourself; saving a package does not transmit it. Email addresses, messages and attachments you send are processed through the email service to respond to your inquiry. Review sensitive recordings and photos before sending them. To request deletion of inquiry messages and attachments, contact the email address above. This document will be updated when features or processing practices change. The effective date and inquiry retention policy will be finalized before public release.

About this website

This static site has no sign-up, inquiry forms, analytics scripts, advertising or externally loaded fonts. GitHub Pages provides hosting and GitHub may process connection information separately from app data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email links open your email app.
